# Motion

The spec for every motion in Devot. A new or changed interaction is classified here before it is built, and this file changes with the code. `tests/policy/motion-tokens.test.ts` enforces the parts a test can read.

It draws on Apple's [Human Interface Guidelines: Motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion), [Adopting Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technologyoverviews/adopting-liquid-glass), [Meet Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/) and [Animate with springs](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10158/). Those give principles, not numbers; the numbers are Devot's tokens.

## Should it move?

Decide in this order; the first match wins.

1. **It follows the pointer or the keyboard directly** (a pane being resized, a drag image, typing): no motion. It is already moving with the hand.
2. **People do it constantly and read the result at once** (another note, another view, palette results while typing, a row's selection): no movement. A colour fade of `motion.duration.quick` at most.
3. **It says where something went or came from** (a sidebar opening, a row or card moving, a pop-up from its button): move it, or fade it when CodeMirror or a remount rules out moving.
4. **It confirms an action on a control** (a press, a switch, a segment): brief feedback on that control only.
5. **It only decorates:** no motion.

Motion never carries meaning alone: the final state must say everything without it. Nothing waits for a motion to end: the new state takes keys, focus and clicks from the first frame.

Native menus and other system-drawn things keep the system's motion.

## Which motion

| What happens                                       | Motion                  | Duration                                         | Curve                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Hover, selection, focus: colour                    | fade                    | `hover`, or `quick` on sidebar and Settings rows | `out`, `quick`                                   |
| Press                                              | shrink to `scale.press` | `quick`                                          | `move`                                           |
| Press released                                     | return                  | `settle`                                         | `spring`                                         |
| Segment's thumb lifted, settling                   | lift, slide             | spring's own                                     | `motion.spring.lift-*`, `motion.spring.settle-*` |
| Two-state change (chevron, section, switch's knob) | turn, unfold, slide     | `toggle`                                         | `move`                                           |
| Pane, row or card changing place                   | slide                   | `slide`                                          | `move`                                           |
| Something growing                                  | size                    | `grow`                                           | `move`                                           |
| Pop-up from an anchor                              | grow and fade           | `popover`                                        | `move`                                           |
| Modal dialog                                       | grow, unblur and fade   | `dialog`                                         | `move`                                           |
| Content replaced in place (List, Board or Gallery) | fade in only            | `popover` or `toggle`                            | `standard`                                       |
| Colour meant to be noticed                         | fade                    | `color`                                          | `standard`                                       |

A diagram redrawn for a new pane width (a gantt chart) has no motion: it follows the pane, and the new drawing replaces the old one once the pane has settled.

## Springiness

`motion.easing.move` has no overshoot and stays the curve for everything that travels. `motion.easing.spring` overshoots by about 3% and is for a press released. A segment's thumb under the pointer uses real springs (`motion.spring.*`, drawn by `springEasing`), with little overshoot. Apple's own controls respond more strongly to touch than to a trackpad, so even this stays slight on a Mac. Never use it on panes, rows, dialogs or menus. Reduced motion turns it off with the rest. Devot follows the system's Reduce Motion by default.

## Glass

A web view cannot bend light, so Devot does not imitate refraction in motion. It keeps what can be kept: a pop-up grows out of the control it belongs to, a pressed control answers in its first frame, and a thumb travels between segments rather than appearing.

## Rules

- **One curve for movement.** Whatever moves, grows, unfolds or turns uses `motion.easing.move`: a critically damped spring drawn as a curve. It is already moving fast in its first frame, so the window answers at once; it settles softly and never overshoots. Ease-in-out (`motion.easing.standard`) is for opacity and colour only; hover and focus keep their own quick curves.
- **Interruptible.** A change that comes while a move is under way carries on from where things are seen, at the speed they have: a slide adds to the one under way (`composite: 'add'`, measured without it by `drawnOffset`), and a fade turns back from how opaque it is. Nothing jumps or restarts from rest.
- **Final state first.** The layout takes its new state at once; the motion plays from the old place to it on top (transforms), and is removed when it ends.
- **Compositor only.** Motion animates `transform` and `opacity`. Widths, paddings and heights change at once, and whatever they move slides there by a transform. A section opening still animates its height (a small area; a known exception).
- **One speed factor.** Every timing comes from `motionTiming.ts`. In a development build and the UI tests' build `localStorage['devot.motionScale'] = '5'` plays all of it (the stylesheet's durations too) five times slower, to watch motion closely.
- **Waiting things wait in place.** A motion that starts after a delay holds its first frame (`fill: 'backwards'`) until it starts.

- Hover, press and selection: a control's fill, colour and edge ease over `motion.duration.hover` (180 ms, `motion.easing.out`); a sidebar row's over `motion.duration.quick` (100 ms, `motion.easing.quick`). A sidebar or note row's hover wash (`color.fill.row-selected-inactive`) is such a fill: it fades in over `motion.duration.hover` on `motion.easing.out`, in the sidebar too, and fades out with the row's own timing; no movement. A focus ring closes in on its control's edge as it comes, its colour and offset over `motion.duration.quick`, and so does a field's edge. Two-state changes (a disclosure chevron turning, a section opening) take `motion.duration.toggle` (200 ms); colour changes meant to be noticed take `motion.duration.color` (400 ms).
- New Note at the end of the note list's search row is a tool: the press (`motion.scale.press`, spring back) and the hover fill over `motion.duration.hover`; moving it from the toolbar adds no motion.
- Hiding or showing the sidebar (or entering focus mode): the pane that goes or comes fades and leaves or takes its room at once, and the panes that stay slide to their new places over `motion.duration.slide` (380 ms, `motion.easing.move`). A toolbar's room for the traffic lights opens at once, and the toolbar items it moves slide to their places with the panes.
- Rows: the sidebar's notebooks and tags that come, go or move fade and slide over `motion.duration.slide` (`motion.easing.move`), and its rows slide 80 px as a workspace opens or closes. A note that comes into or leaves the note list fades in or out where it is, over `motion.duration.insert` (300 ms), while the notes after it slide to their new places over `motion.duration.slide`, as do notes that change order. Only a small change to the list's own data moves (`rowMotion.ts`): another view, scrolling or a section opening never does.
- Sizes: the image viewer between Fit and Actual Size and a progress bar filling move over `motion.duration.grow` (300 ms, `motion.easing.move`); a tag chip just added grows from `motion.scale.chip-enter` as it fades in.
- A toolbar button's tooltip shows after the pointer rests `motion.delay.tooltip` (400 ms); it fades in and out over `motion.duration.popover`, without growing.

- Floating bar buttons (selection toolbar, image size bar): under the pointer and pressed look the same, a `color.line.control` ring over `color.fill.tool-hover`. The text colour eases over `motion.duration.color` (`motion.easing.standard`), the ring over `motion.duration.ring` (`motion.easing.out`); the fill snaps.
- Pop-ups we place ourselves (pickers, tag suggestions) grow from `motion.scale.popover-enter` out of their notch or anchor while they fade in, over `motion.duration.popover`, on their own layer. They rest on the two-layer `shadow.popover-*` shadow in the `color.shadow.popover-*` colours; menus (pickers, tag suggestions) have a 1px hairline edge and `radius.card` corners, and toolbar tooltips a notch pointing at their button.
- The tag menu (`TagMenuPanel.tsx`) is a pop-up we place ourselves at the pointer, or under its row from the keyboard: it grows, fades and folds like a picker, and Reduce Motion takes it to a fade. Choosing a swatch or moving through the menu has no motion of its own: the check is a state, read at once (rule 2). The custom colour panel replaces the menu's content in place, without a transition; dragging on the spectrum or hue slider and typing a hex follow the hand (rule 1), so the handles and previews never animate.
- Quick tagging (`QuickTagPopover.tsx`) is a pop-up from the note's tag row: it grows, fades and folds like a picker, and Reduce Motion takes it to a fade. Its list changes as you type with no motion (rule 2); a tag applied gets the usual chip entrance. On the phone the same list sits in a `PhoneSheet` with the sheet's own motion.
- Completion lists, which CodeMirror places, only fade in, over `motion.duration.fade`: CodeMirror places a tooltip from its measured box, which a growing one would throw off. A code block's copy button grows from `motion.scale.popover-enter` as it fades in, over `motion.duration.toggle`.
- Menus (pickers, tag suggestions) also unfold from the edge they hang from over `motion.duration.toggle`, and fold back as they fade out.
- Modal dialogs grow from `motion.scale.dialog-enter` out of a `motion.blur.dialog` blur as they fade in, over `motion.duration.dialog`; closing, they blur and fade out over the same time while the dim fades out over `motion.duration.backdrop-exit` (400 ms), from as dark as it had become (its fade in lasts `motion.duration.backdrop`), played by an inert copy (`dialogMotion.ts`).
- Closing runs the opening backwards: pickers and tag suggestions shrink to `motion.scale.popover-exit` toward the same notch or anchor while they fade out, over `motion.duration.popover`; completion lists only fade out, over `motion.duration.fade`; floating bars come and go at once, with the selection (`floating.test.ts`). The pop-up itself goes at once, so focus, keys and the accessibility tree move on without waiting; an inert copy that assistive tech does not see plays the motion where it was (`closing.ts`), and a pop-up that opens again replaces its copy.
- Settings > Appearance > Motion > Animations (Always; Follow System, the default; Off) decides whether motion is reduced, shown on the root as `data-motion` (`motionPreference.ts`); every rule below for Reduce Motion reads it (`:root[data-motion='reduced']` in CSS, `motionReduced()` in code), never the system's media query alone.
- Reduced motion sets `--popover-enter-scale`, `--popover-exit-scale`, `--dialog-enter-scale` and `--chip-enter-scale` to 1 and the dialog blur to none (`app.css`), and stops chevrons turning, sections, menus and rows sliding or unfolding, and dialogs' closing copies: everything fades or shows at once, nothing grows, shrinks, turns or slides.

## Interactions

- **Buttons, tools, pop-up buttons, segments, a field's clear button:** shrink to `motion.scale.press` while pressed, and spring back on release. Editor floating bar buttons and rows do not: their pressed state is a fill.
- **Segmented controls:** the pressed look takes its new place at once, and a copy of it slides there on top (`segmentSlide.ts`) on a settling spring. Pressing the pressed segment picks the copy up: it can be dragged to another segment, and the choice applies on release, lifting to `motion.scale.segment-lift` while it is held. A control that remounts on the change gives the place it came from; another view never slides it. A pressed segment does not shrink. A thumb moving between segments of different widths animates its width (a known exception to compositor only).
- **Switch:** the knob widens by `motion.scale.knob-press` while pressed, away from the side it rests on, and stays a capsule. It animates width (small; a known exception to compositor only).
- **Task checkbox in the preview:** shrinks while pressed; the item's colour fades over `motion.duration.toggle`. The strike-through appears at once. A note that opens never plays this.
- **Status dot:** its colour fades over `motion.duration.toggle`.
- **A removable tag pill's x:** rule 2, a hover state.
  Under the pointer or with the keyboard in the pill, the x fades in over `motion.duration.hover` on `motion.easing.out`, and the label's end fades out under it with the same timing.
  The label's fade is a mask whose reach (`--chip-label-cover`, a registered length) eases, a small area and a known exception to compositor only.
  The pill keeps its width either way, so nothing beside it moves.
  Reduce Motion: the x still fades, and the mask takes its new reach at once.
- **Write, Edit, Split and Preview:** a pane that comes slides in from its own side while it fades, and a pane that stays slides to its new place, over `motion.duration.slide`. Widths change at once; a pane that goes leaves at once. Reduced motion: the new pane only fades, over `motion.duration.toggle`. Write and Edit are the same pane: switching between them moves nothing, and the line at the top of the editor stays there.
- **Write Mode button and the view modes' thumb:** Write is the fourth view mode on its own round button before the Edit, Split and Preview group, one choice with them. The button has the Buttons rule (the press shrink); chosen, its icon takes the accent with the tools' colour transition. While Write is chosen no segment is pressed and the group has no thumb: choosing Write drops the pressed look (and a thumb still travelling) at once, and choosing a segment from Write shows it pressed at once, with no slide in either direction, since no segment is there to travel from or to.
- **Write mode's marks:** rule 1. The marks of an element the caret or the selection comes to (a span, a highlight's `==`, a link, a footnote's number, a heading's `#`s, a rule) show at once, and those of the element it leaves hide at once: they follow the keyboard and the pointer, and a fade or slide would move text under the caret. Every other element stays drawn, on the caret's line too: list bullets and numbers, task checkboxes and quote bars are never shown as source. No other line moves: a heading keeps its size and line height either way, a footnote's number is raised by position so its line keeps its height, a quote's bar and a list's indent stay, and a bullet is drawn over its list mark at the mark's own width. Only the revealed element's own line can rewrap, when its marks make it wider. The task checkbox ticks at once, the strike-through with it (rule 2); the folded front matter row opens and folds at once (text folding stays instant).
- **Write mode's emoji and inline HTML:** rule 1. An emoji shortcode and a `<kbd>`, `<sup>` or `<sub>` element show as the preview draws them (the emoji, a key chip, raised or lowered text) and show their source at once when the caret or the selection comes to them, as marks do; every other element of the line stays drawn. Drawn, they never make their line taller, so showing the source moves no other line; only their own line can rewrap. The first time a shortcode or an element is drawn it shows its source until the preview's renderer answers, then the drawing at once, with no fade; later draws come from memory at once.
- **Write mode's code blocks:** rule 1. A code block's fences turn transparent and show again with the selection in it, at once, as marks do; their text keeps its place, so their lines keep their height either way, so no line moves, and the language name on the opening line comes and goes with them.
- **Write mode's blocks:** rule 1. An image, a formula, a diagram or an embed shows rendered on the lines it takes. The caret coming into its source (by a click, or by an arrow from the line over or under it) shows the source at once with the rendering under it, and leaving folds the source at once, with no fade or height animation (it would move text under the caret, and CodeMirror measures the layout). The lines over the block never move; the rendering and the lines under it move down by the source's lines, and an edit draws the rendering again in place. Selecting a block whole (Backspace at its edge, a selection over all of it) rings it at once (rule 2). A double click on an image opens the viewer as any image's does. An image that does not load shows its placeholder at once, with no fade. A block's box keeps the size it had last, or the one its image or diagram is known to have, while it renders, so content arriving does not move the lines around it where that size is known.
- **Write mode's tables:** rule 1. A table always shows as a grid, as the preview draws it, and the caret goes into its cells: its pipes, padding and delimiter row never show, and no line around it moves when the caret comes in or leaves. Typing in a cell, Tab, Shift+Tab and Return, and the alignment, row and column commands redraw the grid at once: a cell grows, a row gets taller and a column wider with the text, and a moved row or column takes its new place at once, with no slide or fade (it follows the keyboard, and CodeMirror measures the layout). Several cursors in a table show its Markdown at once, and leaving one cursor draws the grid again at once. A cell's marks show and hide as Write mode's marks do. The room for the table bar is padding the table always has above its header, so the caret coming in or leaving moves nothing. Column widths include the cells' hairlines, so no header wraps when the grid is first drawn. Another note, another view or a new column width never draws a table without its columns: every row is laid out in its columns from the frame it first shows in. The table bar sits above the grid at its left edge and follows the table as the note scrolls; like the editor's other floating bars it comes, goes and moves at once. The caret's cell, not its row, carries the active line's tint, and a selection that leaves a cell tints every cell it meets whole; both follow the caret at once, and nothing beside the grid or in the bar's room above it is tinted.
- **The panes' top edge (Glass):** rule 2. A scrolled editor or preview fades what lies under its top edge no deeper than it has scrolled, up to `size.scroll-edge`, so a line just under the top keeps its colour until it reaches the edge; the depth eases over `motion.duration.quick` as it follows the scroll, and reduced motion sets it at once.
- **Outline (View > Show Outline):** rule 3, for the column itself: it takes its room at once and slides in from its right edge by `space.9` (`--outline-enter-shift`) while it fades, over `motion.duration.slide` on `motion.easing.move`; it goes at once. Reduced motion: only a fade, over `motion.duration.toggle`. A heading's fold chevron turns over `motion.duration.toggle` like any disclosure chevron and its children show and go at once; choosing a heading and the current-heading mark moving have no motion (rule 2: a fill of `motion.duration.hover` at most); the panes scroll to the heading as any jump does, at once.
- **Note Info card (the note summary, View > Note Info):** rule 3. The card grows from `motion.scale.popover-enter` out of the summary while it fades in, over `motion.duration.popover` on `motion.easing.move`, and closes backwards on an inert copy (`closing.ts`); the summary has the Buttons press. Reduced motion: only a fade.
- **Note Info sections:** rule 3 for the chevron, which turns over `motion.duration.toggle`; the section's rows fade in over `motion.duration.toggle` and go at once, and nothing below slides (their height changes at once). Reduced motion stops the chevron turning.
- **Note Info's date picker:** rule 3. It grows from its date row and closes backwards like the card. A month change (the head's buttons, Page Up and Page Down, an arrow past the month's edge) is rule 3 too: the new days slide 16 (`space.7`, `--month-slide-shift`) in from the side they come from while they fade in, over `motion.duration.toggle`. Moving the day within the month and stepping the time are rule 1: no motion. Reduced motion: the picker only fades, and the days fade in place.
- **List, Board and Gallery:** the new body fades in over `motion.duration.popover`, in the same view only.
- **Gallery cards:** rule 2. Hover washes the card's cell over `motion.duration.hover`; the selection wash and the focus ring are states, shown at once.
  Cards that change place in the order (a new sort, a note added or gone) slide as rows do (`rowMotion.ts`); resizing the pane reflows them at once (rule 1), the sheets' height following their width.
  A thumbnail is drawn when its page arrives, with no fade, and scrolling has no motion of its own.
- **Board cards:** one that changes column flies there on a copy above the columns, as a column would clip it, when both ends can be seen (otherwise it is simply there); the others slide as rows do (`rowMotion.ts`).
  Hover washes a card over `motion.duration.hover` on `motion.easing.out`; the selection wash and the focus ring are states, shown at once (rule 2).
  Resetting the board's width (a double-click or Enter on the edge) takes the fitted width at once, as a drag of the edge does (rule 1).
- **Task ring on a card** (a board card's meta line, a gallery caption): rule 2. The ring and its count show at once when the counts arrive, and a new count redraws the ring at once, without the fill animation of the list's progress bar.
- **Settings:** a section fades in and rises `space.4` over `motion.duration.toggle`, without the rise under reduced motion; its row's fill eases over `motion.duration.quick`.
- **Sync setup and storage:** rule 2. Answering first or another device swaps the question for that path's rows at once, and Change Answer swaps it back; the storage figures and warnings replace their text at once when the relay answers. Nothing is animated beyond the row fill above.
- **Sync through iCloud or a relay:** rule 2. The first question (Sync Through iCloud, Use My Own Relay) swaps for the relay's rows at once, and Use iCloud Instead swaps back; joining, the join question, the fallback's code and recovery key rows, and the vault's rows (transport, storage, devices) replace what was there at once when Rust answers. An iCloud state line and its buttons (Upload Again, Move to This Apple ID, Turn Sync Off, Follow to iCloud, Finish Moving to iCloud, Join, Not Now) change in place with no motion, as a status does. Move to iCloud, Upload Again and Move to This Apple ID confirm in a modal dialog with the dialog motion; Reduced motion follows the dialog's rule. Leave This Vault… swaps its row for the recovery key field, Cancel and Leave This Vault at once, and Cancel swaps it back; typing the key is the confirmation, so no dialog opens, and the vault's rows give way to the first question at once when Rust answers. A relay device not on iCloud yet, or a device of the old key not joined yet after Change Vault Key, is a row of the devices list like the others, and goes at once when a reading of the list no longer has it; Remove on one not joined yet confirms in the same modal dialog as any Remove. On the phone, the attention badge on Sync in Settings shows and goes at once.
- **Change Vault Key in Settings > Sync:** rule 2, as Leave This Vault. Change Vault Key… swaps its row for the relay secret field, Cancel and Change Key at once, and Cancel swaps it back; typing the relay's secret is the confirmation, so no dialog opens. When Rust answers, the row comes back and the new recovery key appears at once, as for a new vault. On a device whose key was changed elsewhere, the note and the join rows replace the pairing row at once, as a status does.
- **Leave This Vault on a relay vault:** rule 2. Leave This Vault… confirms in a modal dialog with the dialog motion (Reduced motion follows the dialog's rule); nothing on the relay changes, so no key is typed. When Rust answers, the vault's rows give way to the joins at once and the sentence that the vault was left is announced.
- **Connect and Disconnect in Settings > Integrations:** each confirmation is a modal dialog with the dialog motion; a client row's state line, its dot and its buttons change in place with no motion, as a value that was set does. Reduced motion follows the dialog's rule.
- **Set Up by Hand in the Clients card:** rule 3 for its chevron, which turns over `motion.duration.toggle`; the configurations show and go at once, as Settings rows do, so nothing below slides. Reduced motion stops the chevron turning.
- **AI Tools in Settings > Integrations:** rule 2. Allow AI Tools and Start in the Background are switches with the switch's own motion; Access is a pop-up; the Notebooks count, the no-access footnote, the Status text, its Quit Devot button and a reachability alert replace what was there at once. Choose… opens the main window's picker as that picker always opens. Quit Devot goes through the quit handshake and the windows close as in any quit.
- **The background menu bar item:** native, placed and removed by the system, with no motion of ours. A Devot that starts in the background shows no window and so plays nothing; its main window, once opened, opens as at launch.
- **Dragging:** a drop line fades in and a column's or row's highlight eases over `motion.duration.quick`.
  The other rows stay where they are: moving them would change what is under the pointer, and the zones that tell before, into and after apart.
  On a board the dragged cards dim to `opacity.card-dragged` at once and stay in their places: a state of the drag (rule 2).
  The column under the pointer takes its `color.fill.track` wash over `motion.duration.hover` on `motion.easing.out`, and loses it the same way; the cards' own column takes none, since a drop there changes nothing.
  The wash stays steady while the pointer crosses the column's cards, header and gaps: only leaving the column's box ends it (`leftColumn` in `board.ts`).
  The drag image is the system's, following the pointer (rule 1): a copy of the card with its count badge, with no lift, scale or tilt of its own.
- **Saved searches in the sidebar:** the section folds as Status and Tags do, and its rows arrive, go and move with the sidebar's row motion. Dragging one shows the drop line before or after a row (the halves of the row, no inside). Running one fills the search field and the results replace the list as a typed search's do; Save Search and Edit Saved Search are modal dialogs with the dialog motion. On the phone the rows are grouped rows in a folding section, and tapping one opens Search with its query, without the keyboard.
- **Notebook icon picker's categories:** rule 2. The grid changes at once, in a box of fixed height; symbols already rendered show in their first frame and the rest fade in where they stand over `motion.duration.popover` on `motion.easing.standard` as their images arrive, painting nothing before. A category's symbols are rendered ahead when the pointer or focus reaches its tab. Reduced motion: they show without fading.
- **Import sheet's wiki links checkbox:** rule 2. A system checkbox: it changes at once, with no motion of ours. The summary's count of links kept as text appears with the rest of the summary.
- **Sync after a restore (Merge or Keep This Mac's Copy):** a modal dialog with the dialog motion (the confirmation's, Merge in Cancel's place); it goes once a choice is made, and the sync status that follows changes in place with no motion. Reduced motion follows the dialog's rule.
- **Open Today's Note:** rule 2. The day's note opens as another note does, and a workspace it is outside goes at once, as leaving a workspace always does; a note just made shows its row as a new note's does. Its Settings rows (General > Daily Notes) are pop-ups with the Settings row fill.
- **An image from the web waiting for Load** (Load Images from the Web off): rule 2. The placeholder shows in the image's place in the preview, in Write mode's image blocks and under its line in the editor; Load swaps every placeholder of that site for its image at once, and the button has the press of any button. Images that arrive later appear as any image does.
- **Image viewer:** opens and closes as a modal dialog does. **Plugin panel:** opens as one and closes at once, because a closing copy would run its frame again.

## iPhone

The springs are `motion.spring.gentle-*` and `motion.spring.bouncy-*`, drawn by `phoneSpring` (`phone/spring.ts`) and set on the phone root. Positions follow the finger and can be interrupted; only selecting and dropping may overshoot; colour and opacity never bounce. A drag follows one finger; if its touch is cancelled, its capture is lost, the app leaves the screen or its screen goes away before the finger lifts, it settles back as a short release does, and the next tap is not swallowed (`phone/dragEnd.ts`). Reduce Motion turns every movement into a `motion.duration.phone-crossfade` crossfade and every scale to 1.

- **Push and pop:** rule 3. The new screen slides in from the right over `motion.duration.phone-push` on `motion.easing.phone-push`; the one below moves 30% left and dims `opacity.phone-dim-push`.
- **Back swipe:** rule 1, then rule 3. The screen tracks the finger; past `size.phone-back-threshold` or on a flick it pops, otherwise it returns.
- **Tab bar lens:** rule 4. At rest the lens is the tab's capsule inset `size.phone-lens-inset` inside the bar on every side, at every tab, its radius the bar's minus the inset (`radius.phone-lens-bar`), so it never reaches past the bar's inner edge and the bar does not clip it. Its two ends move on their own springs, drawn each frame by `phone/tabLens.ts`: the end it travels towards on `motion.spring.phone-lens-lead-*`, the one it leaves on `motion.spring.phone-lens-trail-*`, so it stretches along its travel by width alone and settles back to one tab; neither end goes past the bar's inner edge. A finger on a tab lifts the lens to it with the glass highlight, grown about its middle towards `motion.scale.phone-lens-lift` on `motion.spring.lift-*`. The lift is part of the drawn geometry, not a scale on top: grown, the lens keeps the margin it has at top and bottom (the inset less its growth) from the bar's ends too, so at the first and last tab, held or dragged, it is concentric with the bar at every width, and nothing of it leaves the bar. Moving past 8 pt drags it, centred under the finger through the same springs and held inside the bar, and on release it settles on the nearest tab, which opens with the `selection` haptic; the click that release makes is not a tap, and only that one is ignored. A press released or cancelled anywhere, even off the bar, lets the lens down, and a resize of the bar mid-press cancels it. A change that comes while it moves carries on from where it is, at its speed. Its width animates each frame, a known exception to compositor only: it is one small layer that moves nothing else. The selected icon takes `color.accent-text` and pops over `motion.duration.phone-pop`. Under Reduce Motion the lens moves at once and does not grow; under Reduce Transparency it loses the highlight and stays a plain fill on the opaque bar.

## Local diagrams

PlantUML and Markmap drawings replace their previous SVG at once (rule 2), as Mermaid does. Markmap layout animation, pan, zoom and folding are disabled in the static preview and exported pages.

## Stays instant

Another note; another view; palette results; the editor's floating bars; Write mode's marks showing and hiding; Write mode's emoji and inline HTML drawn or shown as source; Write mode's blocks opening and folding; Write mode's tables redrawn by an edit; a pane being resized; a window's theme or appearance changing (`data-appearance-changing` turns transitions off, because WKWebView can keep half-transitioned layers when a background window changes theme); text folding (it would animate height inside CodeMirror's measured layout).
