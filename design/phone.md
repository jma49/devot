# iPhone layout

The rules the iPhone layout (`src/renderer/src/phone/`) keeps. Tokens are `size.phone-*`, `radius.phone-*`, `color.phone.*` and the `phone` motion tokens, and `tests/tokens/phone.test.ts` holds the numbers.

It follows Apple's [Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines) and [Adopting Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technologyoverviews/adopting-liquid-glass). Where a design value and these rules disagree, the look stays and the behaviour is fixed, as the sections below list.

## Glass

- Glass is on floating controls only: the tab bar, top bar buttons and capsules, the compose circle, the note's bottom bar, the keyboard's accessory bar, menus, toasts and sheets' buttons. Rows, cards and the note itself are never glass, and glass never sits on glass.
- `.phone-glass` is the one recipe: the `color.phone.glass` tint, `blur(10px) saturate(1.9) brightness(1.04)`, a 0.5 edge, inner highlights, inner glow, two drop shadows, a `size.phone-glass-rim` gradient rim (`::before`) and a radial sheen (`::after`). The pseudo-elements are taken: a glass control extends its hit area on a child.
- A pressed glass control grows to `motion.scale.phone-glass-press` and brightens to 1.05.
- The peek's status bar and menu sit over the peek's own 16 px blur and draw no blur of their own: a second one would add nothing, and it would be drawn again every frame they grow.
- Content runs the full height of the screen and scrolls under the glass, with the frosted band under each top bar: `size.phone-topbar-band` below the safe area, `blur(20px) saturate(1.8)`, `color.phone.bar`, a 0.5 hairline in `color.phone.separator`. Content starts `size.phone-content-top` below the safe area. Reduce Transparency makes the band opaque.
- Colour on glass marks what is selected only: the accent on the selected tab, the caret's violet on AI. The compose circle's icon is in the text colour.
- Curves share a centre: a capsule is height / 2, and an inner radius is the outer radius minus the inset. This wins over a design value: fields in the capture sheet are 20 (design 24 and 22). A grouped row's pressed fill is the exception: `radius.phone-row-press` 16 in a 22 card.
- CSS draws the glass (ADR 0010).
- The iPhone has one interface, glass: the Mac's Interface choice (Classic or Glass) is not offered. Glass Transparency is this iPhone's own choice, in `devot.phone.glassTransparency` in the webview's storage, never the Mac's settings and never synced (`phone/glassPrefs.ts`). It moves `.phone-glass`'s tint: -100 is `color.phone.glass-opaque`, 0 the design, 100 half the tint's opacity. Reduce Transparency wins over it.
- Appearance's Light and Dark reach UIKit too: every window takes the chosen style (`overrideUserInterfaceStyle`), so the status bar, the keyboard and the web view's `prefers-color-scheme` follow it, not only the page's tokens.

## Touch

- Every target is at least `size.phone-target` (44 pt). A smaller drawing keeps its size and extends its hit area: the formatting bar's buttons (38 by 40), the sheet's close and capture's Cancel and Save (`size.phone-capture-control`, on a `.phone-hit` child), and the note's chips (30 in a layout item 44 tall, so wrapped rows' targets touch and never overlap).
- A back swipe starts from the left edge and tracks the finger; every push can be undone by it. A vertical scroll never starts a swipe.
- Anything a gesture does is reachable without it: swipe actions and the peek's actions are also VoiceOver custom actions.

## Behaviour

- Each note list section is newest first, so a new note lands at the top of Recent.
- A move of more than 8 pt that is mostly horizontal starts a swipe; a vertical one scrolls and never swipes; a 420 ms hold without either peeks. An open row takes no peek. The peek stays inside the safe area at any text size: its preview gives up its excerpt first, then its menu scrolls, so every action can be reached.
- Every row has a hidden "Actions for…" button that opens the peek's menu, so the swipe and peek actions need no gesture. It stands in for VoiceOver custom actions, which a web view cannot declare.
- A swipe raises the row on a surface (`radius.phone-swipe-surface`, `size.phone-swipe-surface-inset`). Its capsules are `size.phone-swipe-action` by `size.phone-swipe-action-height` with an 11 pt label under each; Pin stays `color.phone.action-pin`, darker than the design's orange so its glyph keeps 3:1.
- Trashing or completing a note (swipe, peek or note info) shows the undo toast for `motion.duration.phone-toast`. Undo puts the note back as it was, its place in the list too (`phone/undo.ts`: a status write moves `updatedAt`, so it restores that as well). A second action replaces the toast and the first stays done. Completing a note already completed does nothing. Only a note's status picker changes a status without one.
- Settings rows open the existing Settings panes in a phone screen. Keyboard, Mirror, Integrations, Local HTTP API, Plugins and Usage (the phone records no usage events) have no row on the phone, and General has one only when the build can send crash reports, its one setting there.
- In a phone screen a pane leaves out what only the Mac has (`useOnPhone`): the Mac's interface style and transparency, the system accent color, the board, activity strip, menu bar, updates, command line tool and folder buttons, Synced Scrolling (there is no split view) and Installed Font (iOS lists no installed fonts to an app; it stays only while it is the choice). Import Font opens the Files picker, which hands Rust a copy of the file. App Font sets the chrome's font (`--font-family-ui` on `.phone-app`), the system font by default; Toolbar, worded Floating Toolbars, is the bars over selected text and tables, not the bar above the keyboard. A short control (switch, stepper, select, push button) sits on its label's row at its natural width, label left, control right; only a free-text field stacks under its label at the row's width. A control too wide for its row wraps under its text, on the right. At the accessibility text sizes a stepper's or select's row stacks too. Messages that name the Mac use the iPhone wording in `i18n/phone.ts`.
- A pane's row carries its label and at most one short description in `color.phone.text-secondary`, only where it adds something the label does not say: about 3-8 words, on one line at 390 pt at the default text size, worded in `i18n/phone.ts` (an empty text there means no description). A description that is the row's value or state may be longer. There are no info buttons. A group has at most a one-line footnote, and a warning before a destructive action stays in its confirmation.

## Accessibility

- Every icon-only button has a label from the message catalog.
- Text keeps 4.5:1 and marks 3:1 (`src/renderer/src/theme/contrast.test.ts`). A gradient theme's backdrop shows behind every screen at `opacity.phone-backdrop`, so page text and marks keep those ratios over every stop (`tests/tokens/themes.test.ts`); its faint text takes `color.phone.text-secondary`, as faint text has no contrast to spare. Colours moved from their design values to meet that say so in their token's description.
- Reduce Motion turns every movement into a `motion.duration.phone-crossfade` crossfade; Reduce Transparency turns glass into `color.phone.glass-opaque`, without its rim, sheen and inner light, and the top band into `color.phone.background`.
- Layout comes from `env(safe-area-inset-*)`, never fixed top and bottom offsets.

## Layout

One page background, `color.phone.background`, on plain and grouped screens.

Top bar: 44 high, `size.phone-topbar-top` below the safe area, 16 from each side. A title is 22 bold, left-aligned at x 20, or after a 44 back circle. On the right, one glass capsule of up to two `size.phone-capsule-button` by 44 buttons: Library has new notebook and search, Notes has filter and search, Settings has pair device and search. The filter button opens a menu of All, Active, On Hold and Done, and turns the accent colour while a filter is on.

Plain lists sit at `size.phone-list-edge` (x 20 to 370), with text at `size.phone-list-text` (52).

Note list: sections Pinned, Recent (not pinned, not completed) and Completed (not pinned, completed); a pinned completed note is in Pinned, and an empty section is left out. Templates and Trash are one flat list. A head (`size.phone-section-head`, drawn 40 in a 44 target) has the title, a mono count, a hairline and a chevron that turns when the section is collapsed. Completed starts collapsed, with a dashed Show more in a `size.phone-show-more` slot, unless the status filter is on Done. Collapsing lasts the session. A saved note opens Recent, and clears a filter that would hide it.

A row is `size.phone-list-row`: a `size.icon-phone-status` status mark at x 20, the title 17 regular with the time in `.phone-figures` 14 `color.phone.text-faint` on the right, then the first tag in its colour, the excerpt 14 on one line and a pin glyph. Rows have no separators.

Grouped pages: cards inset 16, `radius.phone-card`, `size.phone-card-inset` around their rows, no separators. Rows are 44 with `size.phone-row-pad`, and a pressed row fills to `radius.phone-row-press`. A row's value sits beside its label while both fit and goes under it when they don't, so neither breaks a letter at a time; on the Settings root and note info's icon rows it always goes under at the accessibility sizes. The Settings screen and its panes are the exception: they follow Phone grouped lists in `design/layout.md`, with `radius.phone-grouped-card`, separators and titles on the card's 16 keyline.

Library: a card of All Notes, Pinned, Active and Completed, each opening Notes filtered; then Notebooks and Tags under section heads like the note list's, without a count, and collapsing for the session; a card of Templates and Trash; a mono footer. A nested row starts at `size.phone-child-indent`, a further 28 for each level below down to the fourth nested level (the second at the accessibility sizes), where deeper levels line up so a name keeps room to read, with a 1 pt guide through its parent's icon column; an open parent's chevron points down. Counts are in `color.phone.text-secondary` where the design has faint, which keeps 4.5:1 only on the page, not on a card.

Activity: "Week" capsule, then a card of the last 15 weeks (`size.phone-heat-cell` days, `radius.phone-heat-cell`, `size.phone-heat-gap` apart, in `color.activity.level-*`, a month over the week it starts, a less-more legend of `size.phone-heat-legend`); three stat cards, which stack as text grows; then the ranked notes under a section head, their growth in `color.phone.grow`. The capsule's menu offers This week and Last week and changes the stats and the ranking only, never the heatmap. VoiceOver reads the heatmap as one summary, not 105 cells.

Times, counts and stats use `.phone-figures`: mono, tabular figures.

Tab bar: four icon-only tabs (Notes, Library, Activity, Settings) in a `size.phone-glass-bar` glass bar at x 16, as wide as the screen leaves beside the `size.phone-compose` glass circle: 286 on a 390 screen, with the circle at x 312. The selected tab has a lens (inset 4 on every side at every tab, `radius.phone-lens-bar`, concentric with the bar) and a filled icon in the accent; the lens stretches between tabs by width and never reaches past the bar's inner edge, and it can be dragged along the bar; Settings shows a badge when the licence needs attention. The circle is offered on the Notes and Library screens that can take a new note, and hidden on Activity, Settings, Templates, Trash, a note and a pane.

Capture: the circle grows in place into the sheet, `size.phone-sheet-inset` from the screen's sides and bottom, up to `size.phone-capture-height`. The keyboard does not move the layout, so the open sheet rides above it and shrinks to what is left under the safe area, and its contents scroll. A note saved where the list would not show it opens All Notes.

Search: an overlay over the whole screen, including the tab bar. In the top bar's place, a 44 glass field and a 44 close circle. With no query: Recent, then chips (`size.phone-search-chip`, a 44 target) for Notebooks, Status and Tags, each filling the field with `in:`, `is:` or `#`. Recent searches stay in this device's `localStorage`, added on Return or on opening a result; they are never synced or logged. Results are note rows without swipes, found words in `color.phone.match`, the notebook in mono where the time goes, the excerpt moved to start a few words before the match, since one line holds far less than the core's excerpt.

Settings: a card with the app row (dove tile, version and licence state, a badge when the licence needs attention; it opens the trial sheet during a trial and the Licence pane otherwise); General (General, Appearance, Editing, Preview); Sync & storage (Sync); AI & integrations (AI); a card of Licence; a mono build footer. Its search button opens a glass field that filters the panes that open on a phone, by name. There is no Backups or About row: neither has a pane on the phone.

Panes: controls sit in grouped cards, `size.phone-group-gap` apart, laid out as Phone grouped lists in `design/layout.md`: rows of at least `size.phone-grouped-min`, hairlines between them from the 16 keyline, and every value, switch, stepper and chevron on one right edge. A select reads as an iOS row: its value in `color.phone.text-secondary` with the disclosure chevron, cut with an ellipsis rather than wrapped, under the invisible native `<select>` that covers the whole row. A stepper shows its value, then `size.phone-stepper-height` high `size.phone-stepper-button` buttons in a 44 target, its outer corners concentric with the card. A free-text field's radius is concentric with its card. A disabled row's contents fade once to `opacity.disabled`, its switch drawn as an enabled one. A section that would repeat the screen's title (Licence, Preview) has no head, so its first card starts where other panes' do. At the accessibility sizes a row's buttons stack at the row's width under its text, a segmented control stacks its choices in 44 rows. Theme pickers: all six themes in one row of even columns, the same in Light and Dark, each a `size.phone-theme-thumb-width` by `size.phone-theme-thumb-height` thumb (`radius.phone-theme-thumb`) with its sample and accent. The chosen thumb has a ring drawn outside the layout on the thumb's centre, `size.phone-theme-ring-gap` out and `size.phone-theme-ring` wide; a name changes colour, not weight, so choosing moves nothing. At the accessibility sizes the row takes three columns. The text size slider takes its name from its section head, and the small and large A on either side, drawn 30 high around a 44 target. A switch is `size.phone-switch-width` by `size.phone-switch-height`, in the accent colour. A segmented control is `size.phone-pane-segmented` high on `color.phone.lens` in a 44 target, with a `color.phone.elevated` pill; in a card its corners and the pill's are concentric with the card. A card's buttons are full-width action rows under the row's text, labels in the accent colour, wrapped rather than cut. A button that removes something (a device, the AI key, the license) is in `color.phone.destructive`; the Mac keeps them plain and puts the warning in the confirmation. A slider has a `size.phone-slider-track` track with an accent fill and a `size.phone-slider-thumb-width` by `size.phone-slider-thumb-height` thumb in a 44 target. The text size slider keeps the setting's full range, 10 to 32. Sync: the switch and relay with a one-line footnote, then setup rows until enrolled, with the short descriptions of `i18n/phone.ts` and full-width answers, then storage with one footnote (the warning when there is one, otherwise the relay's cap), then Devices ("Added" and the date in `.phone-figures`, a "You" badge on this device instead of the Mac's "This Device", Remove in `color.phone.destructive`) and pairing. The recovery key and every note in a card scale with the text. The recovery key shows only while a new vault's key waits to be saved, as on the Mac; once saved neither has anything to do with it, so there is no row. AI: the provider segmented control and its endpoint, model and key with its state under the field, then next edit suggestions. The theme pickers stay; there are no accent swatches.

Sheets: note info, the pickers, the trial and short messages rise from the bottom edge, the full width of the screen, with `radius.phone-sheet` on the top corners only, since a floating sheet's corners cannot be concentric with every device's. They render at the app's root, over the tab bar and the compose circle; the scrim covers the whole screen. The contents scroll inside, and their bottom padding clears the home indicator (`safe-area-inset-bottom` plus `size.phone-sheet-inset`, at least 16). A sheet is modal: it takes the keyboard when it opens, the rest of the app is inert behind it, Tab goes round its own controls, Escape closes it, and the keyboard goes back to what opened it. A sheet opened over a sheet is the live one until it closes. The tag field's corners are concentric with the sheet (`radius.phone-sheet` less `size.phone-sheet-pad`), and the colour picker's previews cut a long name with an ellipsis and stack at the accessibility sizes.

Detail sheet, for the trial and a failed suggestion: a sheet with a `color.phone.lens` circle holding the info icon, a 20 bold title, the body 16/24 in `color.phone.text-secondary`, and a full-width glass Done, `size.phone-sheet-done` high.

Purchase sheet (App Store builds): its head is the dove in a `color.phone.lens` circle, a 20 bold title and the glass close circle on the trailing edge, a 44 target that closes it as Escape and a drag down do.
The title is Devot Pro, and says the trial has ended once it has.
Under it, three lines of what Devot Pro unlocks, each with a check in `color.accent.text`, in the grouped row's text sizes: 16/23 in the body colour, and under the first, 14/19 in `color.phone.text-secondary`, that reading, search and export stay free.
Then one filled action, `size.phone-sheet-done` high: Try Free while the trial is available, with Buy Once and StoreKit's price as a 44 accent text button under it; once the trial has ended, Buy Devot Pro with the price is the filled action and the trial is gone.
The price always sits in the buy action, and Buy names no price until StoreKit gives one.
Restore Purchases and Enter License Key share the last line, each a 44 target in 15 accent text, with a `size.phone-separator` hairline in `color.phone.separator` between them; Enter License Key and the hairline are there only in a build that takes keys.

Note: a note opens in Preview, and an empty one in Edit, chosen once when its body has loaded. Its text, in Edit and Preview, shares the title's left and right edges. A later change to the body never changes the mode. Back and Forward return to a note in the mode it was left in; leaving the note's screen forgets that. The top bar has a back circle, the notebook's path in mono, and a capsule of the mode toggle and info. Below it the title 26 bold, wrapping to as many lines as it needs (Return adds none), the chips, a long name cut with an ellipsis at the screen's width, then the edit time and word count in `.phone-figures`; there is no per-note sync state. The bottom has a glass capsule of Back and Forward, a find circle and, in Edit, an AI circle with its sparkle in the caret colour. While typing the bottom fades and the formatting bar sits on the keyboard, with a hide-keyboard circle.

Find: the editor's own find, in a glass bar on the keyboard with a count, its numbers in `.phone-figures` and its words in the body font, previous, next and a close circle. It switches a note in Preview to Edit.

Note info: the sheet has the stats in mono, in two columns at the accessibility sizes; icon rows for notebook, created, modified and, on an enrolled device, synced devices, plain rows at full opacity, not buttons, each value under its label at the accessibility sizes; backlinks, which opens the linking notes when there are any; and Move to Trash, with the undo toast. There is no revision history or export row: neither has a phone screen yet.

Undo toast: `size.phone-toast` high, centred, `size.phone-toast-bottom` above the bottom on the tab bar's safe-area logic, above the compose circle. The Undo pill (`size.phone-toast-undo`) reaches its 44 target inside the toast.

The tab bar sits at the larger of `size.phone-tab-bottom` and the bottom safe area less 10, so 24 from the screen's edge at the design's 34 pt inset, clear of the home indicator on any iPhone.

## Dynamic Type

Text follows the system's text size, up to the largest accessibility size.

- Numbers in the specs are at the default size (Large). The scale is the computed size of `font: -apple-system-body` over 17, read at launch and on return to the foreground, and set on the root as `--phone-text-scale`; every font size and line height in the layout is its number times that scale.
- Heights that hold text are minimums, so `size.phone-list-row`, `size.phone-grouped-row`, `size.phone-info-row` and `size.phone-chip` grow with their text. Nothing is positioned by assuming a row height: the list measures its rows, and a saved note's neighbours shift by the height it really takes.
- Text wraps rather than being cut wherever a row can grow. At the accessibility sizes the note list's title may take two lines, a row's time moves under its title, and its preview takes two lines.
- Icons beside text grow with it. Glass bars keep their heights and glyphs, because they sit in fixed places: the top bar's title stops at 1.35.
- The note's editor and preview keep the text size chosen in Settings, and start from the system's scale until one is chosen.

## Icons

SF Symbols only, through `symbol_png` (ADR 0001), monochrome in the text colour. A device is drawn with a generic shape (`phone.devices`), never a symbol that depicts an Apple product. Settings and Library rows have no coloured tile: a `size.icon-phone-row` line icon sits in a `size.phone-row-icon` column, a tag in the same column shows a dot in its colour. A selected tab uses its `.fill` symbol. The synced double tick is `check` drawn twice.

Weights: `regular` at 18 pt and above, and `medium` for chevrons of 14 pt and below, the closest symbol weights to the design's 1.8 and 2.2 strokes.

Status marks are `size.icon-phone-status` symbols in the status colours (`phone.status.*`): active `circle`, on hold `pause.circle`, completed `checkmark.circle.fill` with a white check, dropped `xmark.circle`, no status `circle.dashed`. The Mac's `status.*` symbols stay filled.
