# Notes on Slow Mornings

By *A. Writer*

A slow morning starts with **water**, then [tea](https://example.com/tea).

## What helps

- No phone for the first hour
- A `kettle` that whistles
- Open windows
  - even in winter

> Nothing is urgent before nine.

## Order of things

1. Water
2. Tea
3. Read

***

Written on a Sunday.
